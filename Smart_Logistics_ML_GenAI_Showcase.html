Machine Learning & GenAI Techniques in the Smart Logistics Showcase

AI/ML and GenAI capabilities used for demand forecasting, route optimization, logistics intelligence and continuous learning.

1. LSTM Neural Networks - Demand Forecasting

Deep Learning File: backend/lstm_forecasting.py

  • Stacked LSTM architecture with BatchNorm, Dropout and Dense layers.
  • Uses lag features, rolling statistics and cyclical time encoding.
  • Predicts 7-day demand horizon with confidence intervals.
  • Logistics Value: Enables proactive planning of vehicles, drivers and warehouse capacity.

2. Genetic Algorithm - Vehicle Routing Optimization

Evolutionary Optimization File: backend/genetic_algorithm.py

  • Population-based route optimization.
  • Tournament selection, elitism, Order Crossover and mutation.
  • Optimizes route distance across generations.
  • Logistics Value: Minimizes fuel costs and delivery time.

3. OR-Tools VRP Solver

Constraint Programming File: backend/app.py

  • Google OR-Tools vehicle routing solver.
  • Supports multi-vehicle routing, capacities and time windows.
  • Logistics Value: Production-grade fleet optimization.

4. DBSCAN Geographic Clustering

Unsupervised Learning File: advanced_learning_engine.py

  • Density-based clustering of delivery locations.
  • Identifies natural delivery zones and hub opportunities.
  • Logistics Value: Zone-based dispatching and route grouping.

5. Multi-Objective Optimization & Pattern Learning

Continuous Learning

  • Balances cost, time savings and customer satisfaction.
  • Maintains confidence scores and pattern signatures.
  • Uses operational feedback for continuous improvement.
  • Logistics Value: Smarter decisions without manual retraining.

6. Anomaly & Trend Detection

Statistical Analytics

  • Compares short-term and long-term demand patterns.
  • Detects spikes, drops and emerging trends.
  • Logistics Value: Early warning system for capacity planning.

7. Multi-Agent Architecture

Agentic AI

  • Perceive → Decide → Act → Learn lifecycle.
  • Specialized routing and forecasting agents.
  • Central orchestrator coordinates decisions.
  • Logistics Value: Autonomous and coordinated decision making.

8. Azure OpenAI GPT-4 GenAI Layer

Generative AI

  • JSON-mode prompting and reasoning workflows.
  • Natural-language explanations, reports and forecasting insights.
  • Context-aware logistics assistant.
  • Logistics Value: Explainable AI and conversational analytics.
